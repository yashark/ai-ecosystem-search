This dashboard provides a comprehensive, data-driven view of Turkey's AI startup ecosystem. The entire database — spanning companies, investors, and tracked deals — is autonomously built and maintained by an AI-powered multi-agent system. No manual data entry — every field is machine-generated and continuously updated.
